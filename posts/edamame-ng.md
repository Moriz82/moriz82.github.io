## What it does

- Enumerates Linux and Windows privilege-escalation paths and runs verified recipes.
- Offline catalog of 397K CVE records.
- Strict single-attempt credential-check budgets to avoid lockouts.

## How it's tested

Validated on disposable, isolated Proxmox guests: PowerShell 3.0–5.1, Debian, Rocky and Ubuntu, and Server 2022/2025 Active Directory. Public history is secret-scanned (Gitleaks-verified releases).
