Calendar Sync Terms of Service

Terms for the private local UTSA CyberComp Calendar Sync utility.

Authorized use

This utility is provided only for the account owner's personal, authorized calendar synchronization. The user must have permission to access every connected account and calendar.

Operation

The utility reads the authorized Nextcloud calendars and manages only its marked copies in the authorized Google Calendar. It does not promise continuous availability; synchronization depends on the local Mac, network, Keychain, Google APIs, and Nextcloud service.

User control

The user may stop the local scheduler or revoke Google access at any time. Source removals do not delete Google copies without explicit approval.

The utility is provided as is, without warranties. The owner should independently verify important calendar information and maintain appropriate account security.
