UTSA CyberComp Calendar Sync

A private local utility that mirrors one authorized user's UTSA CyberComp Nextcloud events into their primary Google Calendar.

What it does

The program runs on the owner's Mac. It reads calendar events from the owner's UTSA CyberComp Nextcloud account and creates or updates marked copies in the owner's primary Google Calendar.

Nextcloud access is strictly read only. Google copies are private, contain no guests or conferencing, and never write changes back to Nextcloud. Removed source events are retained until the owner explicitly approves deleting the matching Google copies.

Google access

The utility requests only OpenID identity and permission to manage events on Google calendars the user owns. OpenID identifies the approved account; calendar access is used only to read, create, and update this utility's marked copies in the primary calendar.

Data handling

OAuth credentials stay in the owner's macOS Keychain. Short-lived access tokens remain in process memory. Local private state contains event identifiers, content digests, and audit timestamps needed to prevent duplicates and verify synchronization.

The utility does not sell, advertise with, or share Google user data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
